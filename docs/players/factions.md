# Factions

The powers your character has heard of, and what they'd say about each one. For the places they hold, see the [Galaxy](galaxy.md) page. For how they got here, see the [Timeline](timeline.md).

!!! note "Canon vs. homebrew"
    Each entry is tagged. **Canon** factions are in the Starfinder 2e books, and the books are right unless your GM says otherwise. **Campaign** factions are made up for this game.

## The big three

The powers that decide how this campaign goes. They're covered in more depth in the [Player Primer](primer.md).

### The Swarm
*Canon, bigger than the books describe*

- **What it is:** An insect hive mind that devours worlds and turns what it eats into new weapons.
- **What it wants:** Nobody has found anything it wants besides more. It doesn't negotiate.
- **Where it is:** Advancing out of the Vast. 
- **Who fights it:** The Federation fleet, the Pact Worlds and the Veskarium, none of them well enough.

### The Federation
*Campaign*

- **What it is:** A spacefaring state that runs like a corporation: branded worlds, service contracts, friendly language.
- **What it wants:** Order, progress, and a future in which mortals stop waiting on silent gods to save them.
- **Who it deals with:** Convicts and debtors, mostly. It sentences you to a **debt**, then sends you somewhere to work it off.
- **Who it fights:** The Swarm on the Wall, and the Renegades everywhere else.

### The Renegades
*Campaign*

- **What it is:** A loose fleet of pirates, deserters and free captains.
- **What it wants:** To take back what the Federation took, and to answer to no flag.
- **How they work:** Raids on Federation convoys, prison breaks, then a vanishing act into the Drift.
- **Reputation:** Pirates, to the Federation and its broadcasts. Something else, to the people they free.

## The old empires

### The Stewards
*Canon*

- **What it is:** The alliance of the Sun system's worlds and stations, formed in **41 AG** under the **Absalom Pact**. **Absalom Station** is the capital.
- **What it stands for:** Shared defense, open trade, and a great many species living on top of each other.
- **Recently:** The Drift Crisis added **Dykon, Kalo-Mahoi, Marata and the Diaspora**, and **Pulonis** joined by vote. Aucturn hatched. It's been a bad few years.
- **Relationship with the Veskarium:** Enemies for 200+ years in the **Silent War**, allies since the Swarm hit both in **291 AG**. The peace is uneasy.

### The Veskarium
*Canon*

- **What it is:** The empire of the **vesk**, a warrior people. It's military, expansionist and proud of it.
- **What it stands for:** Strength, conquest and honor won in battle.
- **Recently:** At war with the **Azlanti Star Empire** since the **Battle for Kehtaria** in 324 AG, and losing **Pulonis** to a vote for independence in 325 AG.
- **Relationship with the Stewards:** Reluctant allies against the Swarm.

### The Azlanti Star Empire
*Canon*

- **What it is:** A human empire with a very high opinion of itself. The Azlanti believe they're the true heirs of humanity.
- **Recently:** **Imperator Iorian** was assassinated in 324 AG, and **Imperator Yridela** fled into exile. Succession is a mess, and the empire is at war with the Veskarium.
- **Why it matters:** An empire at war with its neighbor and with itself is an unpredictable one.

### The Starfinder Society
*Canon*

- **What it is:** Explorers, archaeologists and salvagers, founded in **5 AG** by people helping their neighbors on Absalom Station.
- **What they do:** Fund expeditions into the unknown, and occasionally cause diplomatic incidents. See the **Scoured Stars incident** of 317 AG.
- **Why it matters:** Society agents turn up in the places everyone else is avoiding. Some might hire your character, if they're desperate enough.

### The Aspis Consortium
*Canon*

- **What it is:** A ruthless mercantile syndicate. It trades in anything that sells: weapons, relics, favors and people.
- **What it stands for:** Profit, with the law as a cost to be managed. It takes any contract, and it does the dirty work other powers won't put their names to.
- **Who it rivals:** The Starfinder Society, over the same ruins and relics.
- **Why it matters:** Smugglers, mercenaries and con artists will find it hires without asking questions. It also pays what it likes and cuts loose whoever becomes inconvenient.

### The Free Captains
*Canon*

- **What it is:** A loose community of independent pirates, smugglers and free traders. Each captain runs their own ship and answers to nobody else.
- **What it stands for:** Freedom, plunder and the right to go where you like.
- **How it works:** There's no fleet and no chain of command. Captains trade information, hideouts and the occasional favor, and they settle disputes among themselves.
- **Not the Renegades:** The Renegades are an organized fleet with a cause. The Free Captains are a scene. Some captains run with the Renegades, and some wouldn't be caught dead doing it.

## Where your character stands

You aren't aligned with any of these, at least at the start. Before the [Pre-Campaign Meeting](pre-campaign-meeting.md), decide:

- Which power ruined your life, and which one you owe.
- Which power would you trust, if anyone.
