## Your character

The questions in [Session Zero](session-zero.md) go deeper into the colony. These seven cover who the character is overall. Short answers are fine, but specific beats general.

### Background

Where is your character from, and what did they do before the colony? Family, upbringing, profession and what shaped them.

### Motivation

What drives them day to day? What gets them out of their bunk?

### Obligation

Who or what do they owe, and what does it cost them? This can be their balance, a favor, a promise, a family or a debt that isn't on the books.

### Relationships

The people who matter to them: family, friends, rivals, enemies, a lover, someone they'd betray or die for. Name at least **three**, and say where each one is now.
