# Player Primer

What your characters know, or believe they know, at the start of the campaign.

## The galaxy

The campaign uses the standard Starfinder setting: the Pact Worlds, the Veskarium, Drift travel, the Vast, and a past nobody remembers from before **the Gap**. The **Federation** is a homebrew power that sits alongside the canon factions.

!!! note "Canon vs. homebrew"
    Anything in the Starfinder 2e books is true unless your GM says otherwise. The Federation, the Renegades and the colony are made up for this campaign. The Swarm is canon, but in this campaign it's closer, and bigger than the books describe.

## The Swarm

- **What everyone knows:** An insect hive mind that devours whole worlds and turns what it eats into new weapons. It's the largest power in the sector, larger than the Federation and the Renegades put together, and it's advancing.
- **History:** In **291 AG** the Swarm hit the Pact Worlds and the Veskarium at the same time, which forced the two empires to end a 200-year war and ally. A few years ago, heroes broke a Swarm offensive in the Suskillon system and killed the **God-Host**, a Swarm entity that tried to absorb the memories of the shirren exodus.
- **What the broadcasts say:** *"The Wall holds."* The Federation fleet is the only thing standing between the Swarm and everyone else.
- **What the refugees say:** The Wall is falling back. They've seen it.

## The Federation

- **What people see:** A spacefaring state that runs like a corporation: branded colonies, service contracts, friendly language. It builds colonies, keeps trade lanes safe and runs the most disciplined navy in its sector.
- **What it says it stands for:** Order, progress and a future in which mortals stop waiting on silent gods to save them, from the Swarm or anything else.
- **What its convicts know:** Federation justice is fast. It doesn't sentence you to years. It sentences you to a **debt**, and sends you somewhere to work it off.
- **What is really happening** The colonies are covered slave encampment and all of their war effort are bought on sweat and blood 

## The Renegades

- **What people see:** Pirates. Raiders who hit Federation convoys, free prisoners and vanish into the Drift.
- **What they say about themselves:** A free fleet that answers to no flag. They take back what the Federation stole.
- **What is really happening** They pay well, fight dirty for social justice and keep their word.

## Game start 

- You were capture by the federation and your last sight is a massive transport ship.
- All is looking as if you are about to be sent to a colony 

## Who you are

You're independents: smugglers, mercenaries, con artists, disgraced officers, political prisoners, refugees, people who were simply in the wrong place. You might have been framed, or you might be guilty. You might even have gotten yourself arrested on purpose.

## Table expectations

- **Roleplay comes first.** Expect long conversations, hard choices and consequences that carry over.
- **Secrets are welcome.** Give your character things they'd hate the others to learn.
- **Moral gray is the default.** You're pirates. Just choose what kind.
