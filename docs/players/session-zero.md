# Session Zero

Session Zero is the first session of the campaign, and it's **played solo**: one session per character, from your background to the moment the real game starts.

Before that, we hold a [Pre-Campaign Meeting](pre-campaign-meeting.md) to build the party and go through this document. Answer the questions below before your solo session. Some answers stay between you and the GM. Others you'll share with the table.

## Solo sessions

Each player plays one session alone with the GM.

- **Starts** with your background: who you were before your arrest, and how you ended up sentenced.
- **Covers** what comes after the sentence: where it takes you, the balance, the people you owe and the connections you make.
- **Ends** at the start of the group game, with your character at the point where Act 1 opens.

Use your answers to the character questions as the material. The GM will build scenes around them, including the secret, the gear you used to own and the person who might come looking for you.

These sessions are also where the **party questions** pay off: the other PCs can show up as background characters, as someone you've seen around or someone you owe.

## Character questions

1. **Why were you sentenced?** Pick one and tell the GM the details:
    - **Framed:** someone set you up. Who, and why?
    - **Guilty:** you did it. Do you regret it?
    - **On purpose:** you got arrested deliberately. What were you after?
    - **Political:** you said or did something the Federation couldn't allow.
    - **Refugee:** you fled a world the Swarm took, and the Federation billed you for the rescue. Which world, and who didn't make it out?
2. **What do you want more than freedom?** Revenge, money, a person, an answer?
3. **What would you do with a god's power?** Be honest. Nobody else needs to hear this answer.
4. **What secret would you hate for the others to learn?** Tell only the GM.
5. **What's one thing you learned to survive when you had nothing?** A skill, a gang connection, a smuggling trick.
6. **Who on the outside would come looking for you?** And who would be glad you're gone?

## Party questions

Answer these together at the [Pre-Campaign Meeting](pre-campaign-meeting.md):

- Which other PC have you **crossed paths with** but never spoken to?
- Which other PC **owes you something**, even something small?
- Which other PC **do you distrust**, and why?

## Character creation notes

- **Level 1**, standard Starfinder 2e creation.
- Any class and ancestry, but you need a reason to have been sentenced by the Federation.
- You start with **no real gear**: prison-issue clothes, a wrist display, and whatever you managed to keep. Give your GM a list of what you *had* before your arrest; some of it may turn up again.

## Your balance

Choose one with your GM:

| Balance | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Nearly paid** | Months from graduation. You have a lot to lose. |
| **Treading water** | Paying it down slowly, like most people. |
| **Drowning** | It goes up faster than it goes down. Restructuring is close. |
| **Impossible** | A number nobody could ever pay. Someone wanted you never to get out. |

Also decide: **who do you owe off the books?**
- Characters should be **independent**. Don't build a loyal Renegade or a loyal Federation officer. Having been one in the past is fine.

## Lines and veils

We'll agree on these at the Pre-Campaign Meeting: topics that are off the table entirely (**lines**) and topics that can exist but happen off-screen (**veils**).
